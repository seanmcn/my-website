# seanmcn.com

A personal site: writing collected in the library, plus pages for the projects
it describes.

## The library

**Library**:
Everything written on the site: every post, note and find, under `/library/`.
_Avoid_: Blog, archive

**Item**:
One thing in the library: a post, a note or a find.
_Avoid_: Entry, article

**Post**:
An item that is a full article.
_Avoid_: Blog post, article

**Note**:
An item that is a short piece of writing.
_Avoid_: Short post, snippet

**Find**:
An item that passes on someone else's page, with a few lines on why it's worth reading.
_Avoid_: Link, bookmark, share

**Source**:
The page a find passes on.
_Avoid_: Link, URL

**Slug**:
An item's permanent address under `/library/`. Once published it never changes.
_Avoid_: Path, permalink

**Draft**:
An item that isn't published yet.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, hidden post

## Organising items

**Category**:
The single broad subject an item belongs to, such as programming or AI.
_Avoid_: Section, topic

**Tag**:
One of several specific subjects an item touches on.
_Avoid_: Keyword, label

**Series**:
A set of items meant to be read in order.
_Avoid_: Collection, sequence

**Margin**:
A side note shown beside an item's text, made up of a label and a few words.
_Avoid_: Sidenote, aside, callout

**In one line**:
The margin that gives an item's takeaway. An item's summary describes what it covers, and the In one line margin gives what it concludes.
_Avoid_: TL;DR, summary

**Summary**:
One sentence that describes what an item covers. It is shown wherever the item is listed.
_Avoid_: Excerpt, description, teaser

**Featured image**:
The illustration at the top of an item, also shown as its thumbnail.
_Avoid_: Hero image, cover, thumbnail

**Social card**:
The image a page's link shows when it's shared. An item's is built from its title, summary and featured image.
_Avoid_: OG image, preview image, share image

## Projects

**Project**:
Something I've built, with its own page under `/projects/`.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, app

**Written about**:
The relationship from an item to the project it discusses. The item links to the project, and the project lists the item.
_Avoid_: Related project
