Not a new argument, but the framing around interrupt cost is the clearest I've read: a meeting at 9:30 doesn't cost thirty minutes, it costs the whole morning before it.

The version of this I keep coming back to is that the cost is not the meeting, it is the shape it puts on the hours around it. Nobody starts something worth doing at 9:05 if there is a standup at 9:30.
